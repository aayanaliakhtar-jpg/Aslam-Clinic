Conditions & Treatments

Homeopathic care for the full story of your health.

At Aslam Homoeo Clinic, treatment is guided by your symptoms, history, lifestyle, and emotional pattern. This whole-person approach helps Dr. Raja Akhtar Mehmood choose remedies with care.

Chronic conditions are health problems that stay for months or years, often with changing symptoms. Patients may feel tired of repeated short-term relief that does not last.

Homeopathy studies the full pattern: when the symptoms began, what makes them better or worse, how sleep and appetite are affected, and how the illness changes daily life.

Dr. Mehmood takes time to understand your history before prescribing. Patients can expect regular follow-up, gentle remedies, and honest guidance about patience in long-term care.

Kidney stones can cause pain, burning urine, nausea, and fear about surgery. Some stones pass naturally, while others need careful monitoring and medical attention.

Homeopathic treatment looks at the pain location, urine symptoms, stone history, water intake, diet, and report findings. Remedies may help support natural passage and reduce repeated attacks.

Bring your ultrasound or lab reports when available. Dr. Mehmood will guide you about follow-up and will advise urgent medical care if symptoms suggest emergency risk.

Women may seek help for menstrual pain, irregular cycles, weakness, white discharge, mood changes, menopause concerns, or other private health matters.

Homeopathy treats these concerns respectfully by understanding the physical symptoms, emotional changes, family responsibilities, sleep, diet, and long-term pattern.

Female patients are received with privacy and full purdah consideration. You may request that a female assistant be present during consultation.

Anxiety, low mood, stress, sleep disturbance, fear, and overthinking can affect the body as much as the mind. Many patients feel shy speaking about these concerns.

Dr. Mehmood listens without judgment. Homeopathic care considers emotional patterns, life pressure, sleep, digestion, appetite, and physical symptoms together.

For severe depression, self-harm thoughts, or sudden mental health changes, urgent professional help is important. Homeopathy can be discussed as supportive care with proper guidance.

Acidity, gas, constipation, loose motions, IBS-type symptoms, and poor appetite can disturb daily work, prayer, travel, and sleep.

Homeopathy studies food habits, timing of symptoms, thirst, cravings, stress, bowel routine, and what brings relief or discomfort.

Treatment may include remedies and simple routine advice. Dr. Mehmood explains changes in clear words so families can follow them at home.

Eczema, urticaria, itching, repeated rashes, seasonal allergy, sneezing, and chronic allergic reactions often return again and again.

Homeopathy asks why symptoms return, what triggers them, how the skin reacts, and how the patient’s digestion, sleep, weather sensitivity, and stress are connected.

Patients can expect gentle support and follow-up. Long-standing skin issues may need time because the goal is deeper relief, not only temporary hiding of symptoms.

Joint pain, swelling, stiffness, back pain, and reduced mobility can make daily chores and walking difficult, especially for elders.

Homeopathy looks at pain type, affected joints, weather sensitivity, morning stiffness, digestion, sleep, and the patient’s overall strength.

Treatment is planned with patience. Many patients seek support for comfort, movement, and better day-to-day function over time.

Children may need gentle care for cough, cold, allergies, digestion, appetite, sleep, weakness, and repeated minor complaints.

Homeopathic care studies each child’s nature, appetite, fears, sweat, sleep, and pattern of illness. Adults and elders are treated with the same careful attention.

Parents are guided in simple language. For emergencies, high fever, breathing trouble, dehydration, injury, or severe weakness, urgent medical care should not be delayed.
