About the clinic

A respected local doctor serving families for over three decades.

Doctor profile

Dr. Raja Akhtar Mehmood

With 35+ years of clinical experience, Dr. Mehmood has treated generations of families from Banian, Kharian, Gujrat, and nearby villages.

His approach begins with time and attention. Instead of rushing to a medicine name, he studies the full case: symptoms, triggers, lifestyle, appetite, sleep, stress, family tendencies, and previous treatment history.

This patient-first method helps build confidence, especially for chronic illness, female health concerns, kidney stone complaints, and mental health support.

Healing philosophy

Natural care with practical judgment

Homeopathy looks at the person, not only the disease label. Dr. Mehmood believes lasting improvement comes from understanding the pattern behind illness and supporting the body's own healing response.
