Homeopathy Explained

Simple answers about natural homeopathic care.

Many families first come to homeopathy after trying several treatments. This page explains the approach in plain words so you can ask better questions and feel more confident.

What Is Homeopathy?

Homeopathy is a system of natural medicine that uses carefully selected remedies in very small doses. The remedy is chosen according to the patient’s full symptom picture, not only the disease name.

A good homeopathic consultation asks about physical symptoms, sleep, appetite, thirst, weather sensitivity, fears, stress, past illness, family history, and the pattern of the complaint.

Is Homeopathy Safe?

Homeopathic remedies are gentle and are commonly used for children, adults, and elders. They are not meant to replace emergency care or essential treatment for serious conditions.

Dr. Mehmood reviews each patient’s situation and advises when reports, tests, or urgent medical care are needed.

How Long Does Treatment Take?

The honest answer is that it varies. Some acute complaints may improve quickly. Chronic illness, old skin problems, arthritis, kidney stone tendency, or hormonal concerns usually need patience and follow-up.

The aim is steady relief and better balance, not hurried promises.
