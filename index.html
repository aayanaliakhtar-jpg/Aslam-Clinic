About Dr. Mehmood

Care shaped by patience, experience, and trust.

Aslam Homoeo Clinic is a family-focused homeopathic clinic in Banian, Kharian, Gujrat, led by Dr. Raja Akhtar Mehmood with over 35 years of experience. The clinic provides natural, affordable, and personalized treatment for chronic diseases, kidney stones, female health concerns, mental health issues, and general family care — serving the local community with trust, compassion, and lasting commitment.

Dr. Mehmood begins with listening. He asks about symptoms, daily habits, diet, sleep, stress, emotions, and long-term health patterns so the remedy is chosen for the whole person.

His practice is rooted in village life and community responsibility. Patients are received with dignity, fees are kept affordable, and families are guided with honest expectations.
